How ranking works

Every eligible candidate gets a priority score built from four parts:

  • HLA match — 12 points per avoided mismatch, out of 6 possible.
  • Time waiting — 1.5 points per month on the list, capped at 100.
  • Medical urgency — the 0–100 urgency score × 0.6.
  • Pediatric bonus — a flat +40 for pediatric candidates.

Before scoring, a candidate is checked for two hard stops that override any score: an ABO-incompatible blood type, or a PRA of 80% or higher (a stand-in for a high-risk crossmatch). Either one moves the candidate to "on hold" instead of the ranked list. These weights are a simplified stand-in for this project — not a real clinical allocation policy.
